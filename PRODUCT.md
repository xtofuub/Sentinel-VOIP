# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Security researchers, protocol analysts, and operators reviewing a reconstructed VoIP workflow in a browser UI. They need to browse scenario data, configure controlled test runs, and inspect recorded outcomes without losing trust in what the interface is doing.

## Product Purpose

Sentinel-VOIP presents a research-oriented VoIP scenario catalog and dashboard. The interface should make complex signaling, locale selection, previews, task status, and recordings feel legible, deliberate, and bounded.

## Brand Personality

Precise, nocturnal, controlled. The product can feel cinematic and high-signal, but it should not feel reckless, toy-like, or hostile to the user.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic neon hacker dashboards, decorative glassmorphism, prank-store gimmicks, gradient text, heavy side-stripe card accents, and anything that makes controls feel less trustworthy. Avoid copy that celebrates harm or obscures responsibility.

## Design Principles

Reveal the system state clearly before asking for action.
Use atmosphere to support focus, not to hide information.
Make every interactive control feel predictable and keyboard-accessible.
Keep the visual language restrained enough for a real tool, with accent color reserved for selection, status, and primary actions.
Use research-oriented language that keeps the experience bounded and responsible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, minimum 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, and copy that does not rely on color alone to communicate status.
